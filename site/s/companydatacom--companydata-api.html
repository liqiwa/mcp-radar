About companydata-api

The CompanyData Company Data API is a company and business data API for KYB, enrichment and lead generation, built on official government registers: 377M+ registered businesses in 190+ countries, from 1,200+ registers and sources, in one schema and refreshed monthly. Search a company by name, registration number, address, phone or email, then get the full record: address, registration and VAT number, industry codes, employees, revenue, contacts, executives and the corporate structure (parent, ultimate parent and every company in the group).

One API key covers every country. On the same key: domain enrichment (website in, company out) and location lookups.

- Docs: companydata.com/api-docs - KYB / business verification: companydata.com/kyb-api - OpenAPI spec: companydata.com/openapi.json - Get an API key: app.companydata.com/signup-api (7-day trial with 500 searches and 10 exports) - Sample data: 547 Dutch software companies in this repo, plus free city datasets (CSV, no key needed) - Runnable examples: bulk file, API and MCP

- KYB and onboarding: check that a business exists in the official register, with its registration number, legal form and address. Due diligence & KYC guide - Company search and autocomplete: a company name field in a signup or checkout form that fills in the registered address. Autocomplete guide - CRM and lead enrichment: complete a record from a name, registration number or website domain with industry, size, revenue and contacts. Matching a list - Corporate structure: every company in a group, from any member, with local, national and global headq
