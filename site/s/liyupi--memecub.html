About memecub

Meme search and automatic meme placement for AI agents.

Give Claude Code, Cursor, Codex or Claude Desktop a meme library. Search reaction memes by feeling, or hand over an article and get it back with the right meme after the right sentence.

Real output of matcharticle. The model picks the moments; memecub finds the meme and inserts it as Markdown.

- It places memes, not just finds them. matcharticle reads a whole post, picks the sentences worth a meme and returns the article with images inserted. Your agent doesn't have to guess where they go. - Search by feeling. Ask for facepalm, this is fine, it works on my machine, 无语 or 摸鱼 and get the reaction you meant, not a keyword lookalike. - English memes and Chinese 表情包 in one library. Drake, This Is Fine, Doge and Surprised Pikachu next to 熊猫头, 奶龙, 蘑菇头, 金馆长 and DeepSeek 鲸鱼娘. Chinese queries match whole phrases, so a character name returns that character. - Zero install. A remote MCP server over Streamable HTTP, or a plain-curl Agent Skill. Searching works without a key. - License-aware results. Every image says whether it is cleared for commercial use, links its source, and ships a ready Markdown snippet with the credit line when one is required. Ask for commercial-safe only and that's all you get. - Safe defaults for agents. Memes whose captions contain swearing are left out of agent results unless you ask for them.
