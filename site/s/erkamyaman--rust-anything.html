About rust-anything

Rewrite your whole app in Rust, one piece at a time, proving nothing changed. For CLIs, HTTP servers and libraries in TypeScript/JavaScript, Python, C, Go, Ruby and Java: rust-anything ports your app piece by piece, checks every step against the original's recorded behavior, and hands you a pure Rust app. Only need the slow part fast? Port just that, one hot path at a time:

Real workload, same results as the original Original With rust-anything --- ---: ---: x18n's sidebar search, 50,000 keys (TypeScript, Node) 10 ms per query 0.5 ms: 20x faster The same search in the browser (wasm, one thread) 10 ms 0.95 ms: 11.5x slugify over 20,000 strings (Python) 98 ms 17x faster Shellwords.shellescape from Ruby's standard library, 50,000 values 88 ms 11.8x faster Slugify over 50,000 strings (Go) 49 ms 4x faster slugify over 50,000 strings (Java) 43 ms 3x faster

Measured on 4 cores; every number has a benchmark in demo/ that first checks the results are identical. Calling Rust once per small function is usually slower than a JIT; rust-anything finds the loop worth moving, and moves it.
