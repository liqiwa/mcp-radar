About free-crypto-trading-bot

Four backtested 4h trend-following strategies for crypto futures, an open backtest you can re-run yourself, a web panel and 11 exchanges. Free.

Русская версия · Guide · Research · Agent API

Risk warning and disclaimer. This is high-risk trading: perpetual futures can lose your entire deposit. BYTtrade is not responsible for the results you get or for any losses. Backtest and paper-trading results do not guarantee future returns. Nothing here is investment advice. You act at your own risk. The software comes without any warranty; see sections 8 and 9 of the LICENSE.

The panel with demo data: every balance, position and trade in the pictures on this page is invented.

- 📈 Four strategies with an open backtest. Trend following on 4h candles for 20 large coins, chosen and checked on 3.5 years of history (2023–2026) with fees, slippage and funding. A later re-test on 2020–2022 came out weaker; both are shown below. Two commands recompute the 2023–2026 table on your computer. - 🖥️ A web panel instead of a config file. Start and stop, positions, trades, settings and exchange keys in the browser, on a phone too, in English and Russian. - 🛡️ Built to limit damage. Every position gets a stop-loss on the exchange, an order is never sent twice, and a real account starts only after you type a confirmation phrase. - 🔌 11 exchanges, demo first. Binance, Bybit, OKX, BingX, KuCoin, Hyperliquid, Bitget, Gate, MEXC, HTX, BloFin. Start on a demo account where the exchange has one. - 💸 Free. No subscription and no paid tier.
