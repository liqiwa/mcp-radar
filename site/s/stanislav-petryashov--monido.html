About monido

English · Русский · Deutsch · Español · Français · Português · 中文

Track your net worth and cash flow, on your own or as a family. Self-hosted, entered by hand, no bank connections.

Website · Live demo · Help build Monido · Releases

Try the live demo: a fictional family in a private copy for 15 minutes, nothing to install; the sign-in screen shows the password.

- How much do we own, after debts? Accounts, investments, property and loans of every family member, in every currency, in one figure. Example - Did we get richer this year, or did prices and exchange rates move? Net worth over time, and its real growth after inflation in the currency you spend in. Example · formula - How much did we save ourselves, and how much grew on its own? Savings and the savings rate per month, and the growth beyond them. Example · formula - How long would we last without income? The emergency fund in months of spending. Example · formula - When do we reach our goals, and can we afford this? Goal dates from what you actually save; with the optional Claude advisor, a purchase or a loan weighed against your own numbers and sent to the family for a vote. Goals · A family decision

You need Docker with Docker Compose v2.24 or newer (Linux, or Docker Desktop on macOS). In a new folder:

Open http://127.0.0.1:8080 within 15 minutes and set the family password. Server settings (port, address) go in an optional .env (.env.example, the full guide). To update: docker compose pull, then docker compose up -d. Something not working? Troubleshooting.
