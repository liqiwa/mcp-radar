About konstruct

The open context layer AI agents can trust.

A self-building ontology of your business, read from the code you already have, with its SQL lineage proven against your data. Open source, local first, one command.

Documentation &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; Getting started &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; GitHub Action &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; Commands &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; Changelog &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; Paper &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; Manifesto

AI agents are confidently wrong about your business. 57% of the enterprises surveyed had traced a confident but wrong answer from an AI agent to missing or inconsistent business context in the previous six months (VentureBeat, 2026, a VB Pulse survey of 101 enterprises in June 2026). An agent sees tables and columns. It does not see that revenue has an approved definition with a required filter, that finance and marketing compute it two different ways, or which dashboards its change is about to break.

The answer taking shape is a context layer: a model of the business that agents read before they act. Microsoft (Fabric IQ ontology over MCP), Snowflake (Horizon Context) and Databricks (Unity Catalog semantics) are each building one, each as part of its own platform, and Palantir's Ontology models a business as objects, properties and links inside Palantir's proprietary platform.
