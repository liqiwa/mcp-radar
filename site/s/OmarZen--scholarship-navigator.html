About scholarship-navigator

Find the scholarships you can actually win, and never miss a deadline again.

Eligibility checks · match scores · missing papers · a dated plan · essay drafts Free, open source, and built to work inside your AI assistant.

Quick start · Use with AI · Features · What's covered · Contribute · العربية

Every year, thousands of strong students miss fully funded scholarships. Usually they were good enough. What went wrong was something else:

- they found out about the scholarship after the deadline; - they spent weeks on an application they were never eligible for (wrong nationality, age or field); - they were one paper short: a missing recommendation letter, an expired IELTS, a transcript without apostille; - the information was spread across hundreds of outdated blog posts and WhatsApp forwards.

Scholarship Navigator fixes that. Tell it about yourself once. It checks every rule, scores how well you match, lists exactly which papers you're missing, and builds a plan with real dates, backwards from each deadline.

You: I'm Egyptian, BSc in electronics engineering, GPA 3.58/4, IELTS 7.0, 3 years of work. I want a fully funded master's in AI or embedded systems in Europe, starting 2027.

Your AI assistant (using Scholarship Navigator): - ✅ You're eligible for 22 of 35 scholarships. ❌ 13 aren't open to you, and here's why (nationality list, age limit, field). - 🥇 Erasmus Mundus: fit 98/100, papers 56% ready, deadline 15 Jan 2027 (estimated, verify). Start your motivation letter by 9 Dec. - 🧭 Programs that fit you: DEAI (Belgium · Spain · Austria · France · Italy), EDISS (Finland
