About codex4ever

Codex4ever — Codex quota & reset planner

If you find it useful, a ⭐ star helps more people find it.

Runs in your browser · no sign-in · or ask Codex with the plugin

Codex4ever is a planner for your OpenAI Codex weekly quota. It shows when your quota runs out at your current pace, whether a predicted extra reset is likely to arrive first, and when a banked reset is worth redeeming.

Unofficial; not affiliated with OpenAI. MIT licensed.

Ask once in Codex and the planner opens beside the chat. Pick a strategy and the plan changes: daily usage, refills and run-out points.

- Timeline of your next two weeks — daily usage, remaining quota, run-out points, outages, normal refreshes and banked-reset uses, one column per day. - Extra-reset forecast — the chance of an automatic reset in the next 48 hours, the likely window, and a second reset conditioned on the first, built from public reset history and Tibo's public posts. - Five strategies — Keep pace (your current pace: see when quota runs out), Keep available, Use it all (empty quota just before the next refill), Weekdays only, Weekend focus. - Codex plugin — reads your local, signed-in quota and banked resets, plans in the conversation, and opens the same visual planner.

- A predicted reset is counted only when it is at least 60% likely before the refresh in effect, and it is placed at a conservative time (where it has most probably already happened), not at its single most likely hour.
