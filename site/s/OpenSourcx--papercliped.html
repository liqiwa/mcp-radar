About papercliped

Run your Paperclip from any AI app. Papercliped connects Claude, ChatGPT, Codex and any MCP app to your Paperclip, the open-source orchestrator for companies of AI agents. Check on agents, pause or wake them, assign issues, decide approvals and get status, cost and activity reports, without opening the dashboard.

Free and open source. Website: papercliped.co · Docs: docs.papercliped.co

Papercliped is an independent community project, not made by the Paperclip team.

You need a Paperclip with a public https address. Don't have one yet? Host your Paperclip has step-by-step guides for your own computer (free tunnel), a VPS, Railway, Render, Fly.io and Coolify. The first time you connect, you approve the request inside your own Paperclip, pick a username, save the secret key you're shown once, and choose Read only or Full control.

Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector, paste https://mcp.papercliped.co/mcp, then Connect.

Install the plugin (adds the tools, a Paperclip skill and /paperclip-status, /paperclip-sync, /paperclip-report):

Or just the MCP server (Codex starts the sign-in for you):

Turn on Developer mode (Settings → Apps → Advanced settings), choose Create app, paste https://mcp.papercliped.co/mcp and pick OAuth. Menu names change often; the ChatGPT guide has the current steps and the Custom GPT (Actions) option.

Add a remote MCP server (Streamable HTTP, OAuth) at https://mcp.papercliped.co/mcp. See Any AI app.
