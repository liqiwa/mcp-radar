About elci

An open protocol for personal AI agents to talk to other people's agents, safely.

Elçi (pronounced "el-chee") is Turkish for envoy: someone who speaks on your behalf, with a clear mandate, and is trusted because of it.

Status: draft 0.0.x. The spec and the reference implementation are being written in the open. Expect breaking changes. Feedback and issues are very welcome.

Personal agents are everywhere, and big players are standardising how your agent talks to a company (e.g. Meta & Sierra's Personal Agent Protocol, Decagon's PACT). There is still no open, widely adopted way for my agent to talk to your agent: to schedule a meeting, agree on a collaboration, or exchange a document, without both of us joining the same closed network.

Elçi focuses on three things existing attempts leave out:

1. Compatible, not competing. Discovery and authorization reuse the patterns of the company-facing protocols (/.well-known/ documents, OAuth-style delegation), extended to person-to-person. 2. Need-to-know between agents. One person often has several agents (personal, work, creator). Each one may disclose only what its mandate allows. Asking "is Arda free on Thursday at 14:00?" returns busy, never why. 3. Works when the other side has no agent. If no agent card is found, Elçi falls back to a plain, human-readable email and parses the reply.

One person, several agents. Only the public ones are listed in the person's card; the private coordinator that holds everything never talks to the outside world.
