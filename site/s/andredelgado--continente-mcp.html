About continente-mcp

An MCP server that lets AI assistants shop at Continente.pt: search products, check prices and sale rules, read your favorites and past orders, and fill your cart. Checkout and payment always stay on the website.

Unofficial. This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by or supported by Continente or MC Sonae. It automates the public website with a headless browser, may break when the site changes, and should be used in line with the website's terms. You are responsible for anything placed in your cart.

- Catalogue search and product details, with exact prices, unit prices, availability and sale rules for weighted and bulk products. - Safe cart changes. Every change is confirmed by reading the cart back. If a change cannot be confirmed, the result says so and tells the assistant to check the cart instead of retrying. - Your account: favorites, order history and a ranking of the products you buy most. - Runs locally over stdio. Your credentials and session are stored on your computer and sent only to Continente.pt. - Built-in guidance. The server tells the assistant the cart rules as soon as it connects, and offers a basket-planner prompt with the full shopping workflow. Nothing extra to install; the same guide also ships as a skill.

- A Continente.pt account for account features (search and product details work without one) - Claude Desktop on macOS or Windows for the one-click install, or Node.js 22 or newer for any other MCP client
