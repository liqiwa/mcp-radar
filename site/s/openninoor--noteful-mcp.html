About noteful-mcp

Let an AI agent read and write your Noteful notebooks.

An MCP server for the iPad note-taking app Noteful. It sees your pages the way the app draws them, knows what you highlighted and what you hid under tape, and writes new pages in real, editable Noteful ink: handwriting, highlighter, hand-drawn diagrams. Everything runs on your own computer.

Beta. Noteful's format is reverse engineered. Reading never changes your library and writing only creates new files, but expect rough edges, and please report pages that read or render wrong.

- Read every notebook of your library live (with Noteful for Mac) or from exported .noteful files: page images, typed and PDF text search, highlighted passages, taped answers. - Write notebooks and pages from Markdown in natural handwriting, annotate existing pages, and draw diagrams. The result is real Noteful ink: lasso it, recolour it, erase it. - Study notes look: composed summary pages and dark tree pages with titles, cards, columns, colour zones, sticky notes and pastel-coloured diagrams the agent draws as SVG. - Copy a page design: have an image model draw the page, then the server copies it into ink, drawings included: same layout, sizes and colours, correct words (optional, with your own Google key). - Science-ready handwriting: Greek letters, super/subscripts, arrows and math signs. - Private by design: no cloud service of ours; nothing leaves your computer unless you turn on mockups. Your library and source files are never modified.
