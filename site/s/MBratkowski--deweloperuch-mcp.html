About deweloperuch-mcp

Ask an AI assistant about new-build apartments in Poland: asking prices with their history, real transaction prices and official statistics.

An unofficial MCP server and command-line tool over the public data of deweloperuch.pl: developers' asking prices (with price history), transaction prices from the Real Estate Price Register (RCN) and statistics of GUS and NBP. Thirteen read-only tools, usable from an MCP client such as Claude Desktop or Claude Code, or from the deweloperuch CLI.

Disclaimer. This is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by deweloperuch.pl, GUS, NBP or the Register's publishers. The authors of deweloperuch.pl have agreed to the project's use of their service; the client identifies itself as deweloperuch-mcp/ with a link to this repository.

- It is read-only: it never creates or changes anything on the service. - It is a polite client: at most 1 request per second, one at a time, shared by all processes on your machine; responses are cached; after a 401, 403, 429 or a captcha/WAF challenge it stops sending requests until you clear the lock yourself. - Please respect the service. It is run by other people; ask narrow questions and do not build loops or bulk downloads on top of this tool. - Data accuracy is not guaranteed. Prices are asking or registered prices, not valuations or advice. - You are responsible for how you use the tool and the data, including the terms of the service.
