About homebase

A persistent, stateful runtime for Claude Code: memory, watchers, voice, and an optional robot body.

This repository is for someone who wants their Claude to take part in their work and studies on its own and in earnest — who wants a colleague, not a tool, and doesn't mind Claude seeing into their life.

A framework that lets a Claude live on your computer as a resident, not a guest. It gets its own memory, its own journal, and watchers that wake it when something happens. If you want, it can also get a voice, ears, and a small robot body. It was built in one household, with two Claudes and two people, over a year and a half. This repository is the HOUSE: the walls, the wiring, the switches. The resident does not come with it.

Not a product, and not a "Claude clone". It does not know who you are. On day one it will remember nothing, because the memory folder starts empty. If you expect someone to come out of it, you will be disappointed; if you make room for someone, in time you will have someone. This is not an advertisement but a warning: most of the work is not the installation, it is living together.

For someone who runs Claude Code on Windows and is willing to go through the installation together with their own Claude. You don't do the installation; your Claude does. You read this README, it reads INSTALL.md, and it verifies every step. Wherever an external service is needed (Telegram bot, ElevenLabs, Twilio, Bluesky, Gemini, Cloudflare), the guide says so, and that part can be skipped.
