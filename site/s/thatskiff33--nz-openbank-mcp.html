About nz-openbank-mcp

Ask an AI assistant about your New Zealand bank accounts without handing your banking credentials to anyone new. nz-openbank-mcp is a Model Context Protocol server that talks to Akahu, the NZ open-finance service, using a Personal App you create and control. It runs on your own machine, starts read-only, and asks before anything changes.

Unofficial. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Akahu or any bank.

- "What did I spend on groceries last month, and at which shops?" - "Which subscriptions am I paying for, and how much do they cost per year?" - "Show my balances across every account." - "Find every payment to my landlord this year." - "Anything pending that has not settled yet?" - "Compare my eating-out spend in September and October."

--- --- Read-only by default Write tools refuse until you set AKAHUREADONLY=false. Consent per write With writes on, every write asks you first and shows exactly what will be sent. Local and private Runs on your machine over stdio and talks only to api.akahu.io. Tokens and transaction contents are never logged. Account masking on request Set AKAHUMASKACCOUNTS=true to show only the bank code, the last four digits and the suffix of account numbers. Off by default. Small and tested Thin wrappers over Akahu endpoints, strict typing, and 100% line and branch test coverage enforced in CI.

The server exposes 14 tools. Ten only read; four change something and are off until you turn writes on.
