About gmcoach

An AI coaching plugin for the Speediance Gym Monster (GM1 and GM2). It plans strength training in Codex, ChatGPT and Claude from your own workout history and current research.

- Training plans: creates Gym Monster programs for strength, timed exercises and cardio, using only exercises and accessories you confirm, with a matching warm-up and stretching. - Multi-week cycles: plans progression and deloads week by week, with dates, and saves the whole cycle after one approval. - Smart RM and resistance modes: uses the Gain Muscle, Stamina and Strength presets and the Standard, Chain and Eccentric modes, or a fixed load suggested from your 1RM. - Editing saved workouts: changes rest, resistance mode, load or exercise order and shows the differences before saving. - Calendar: reads your Speediance calendar and schedules saved workouts on chosen days. - Session length: estimates how long a workout takes and fits the plan to the time you have.

- Workout history: reads completed sessions with sets, loads, heart rate, rowing data and period statistics. - Session comparison: compares two completed sessions set by set. - Recovery data: reads Speediance health reports such as sleep, HRV, fatigue and muscle load, with your permission.

- Training profile: a short interview records your goals, preferences and limitations locally. - Injury-aware plans: takes reported movement limitations into account. - Equipment: checks your accessories and what each exercise needs, and searches the Gym Monster exercise catalogue. - Research-backed decisions: searches PubMed and links real sources to pl
