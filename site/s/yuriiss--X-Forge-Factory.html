About X-Forge-Factory

An operator console for Magnific — every capability on one surface, with an engine that treats credits like money.

X-Forge talks to Magnific over both of its surfaces at once: the REST API at api.magnific.com with your own key, and the MCP server at mcp.magnific.com over OAuth. Between them you reach the full catalogue — 48 image models, 52 video models, ~600 voices, 88 MCP tools — instead of the handful any single surface exposes.

Behind the seventeen screens sits a job engine built to a specification rather than to a demo: credits are reserved before work starts, charged exactly once when it finishes, gated behind a human when the estimate is large, and never spent twice on work that may already have completed.

Then open MCP Console → CONNECT and sign in once. Without it the console still generates images and video on the REST key, but you lose the balance, cost estimates, the full catalogue, text-to-speech, 3D and most of Edit Suite.

New here? GUIDEBOOK.md walks through every screen and every control, with screenshots — also built as a printable PDF at docs/X-Forge-Guidebook.pdf.

Українською: GUIDEBOOK.uk.md · PDF. The console itself speaks both languages — the picker sits in the topbar.

Nothing in the console is mocked. Every panel is reading something.
