About carejobs-mcp

A free, hosted, read-only Model Context Protocol server for US healthcare jobs. It lets Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code and other MCP clients search the open jobs that CareJobs.Now reads every day from the career sites of 258 hospitals, health systems, clinic groups and care companies (170,000+ openings), with the pay each employer posts. Every answer links to the job's page on CareJobs.Now, where the user applies on the employer's own site.

- Endpoint: https://carejobs.now/api/mcp - Transport: Streamable HTTP (legacy clients that open with initialize are served too) - Auth: none. Public, read-only, no API key, free. - Official MCP Registry: now.carejobs/healthcare-jobs - Docs and data: https://carejobs.now/data

Tool What it does --- --- searchhealthcarejobs Search open US healthcare jobs by words (a unit, a shift, a town), kind of job, state, shift and pay. Returns title, employer, kind of job, posted pay, shift, place and a link to the job. gethealthcarepay Posted hourly pay by kind of healthcare job (median and typical range, with the number of rates and employers behind it). gethealthcarejobmarket How many healthcare jobs are open, how many were first listed in the last 7 days, and who is hiring. gethealthcarejobsbystate Open jobs and employers per US state, or for one state by kind of job. listhealthcareemployers The hospitals, health systems, clinic groups and care companies whose jobs CareJobs.Now reads. gethealthcareemployer One employer: what it is, where it hires, its open jobs and the pay it posts.
