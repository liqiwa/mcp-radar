About astro-jolie-plot

Paper mode: serif text, Computer Modern math, grey four-sided frame, inward ticks. Synthetic data.

Claude skills that make jolie figures for astrophysics, then check that a stranger reads them correctly. Two skills ship together:

- astro-jolie-plot sets the style: a restrained publication look, curated palettes, and one rule for what a plot is for. - figure-reviewer checks the result. A Claude model that sees only the pixels says what the figure shows, and you compare that with what you meant.

The problem: you cannot review your own figure. You know what it should say, so your eye finds the message even when the figure does not carry it.

The fix: hand it to a blind reader. If the reader's one-sentence answer differs from yours, the figure is wrong.

Approach What it catches What it misses --------- Reread your own figure Typos, unit slips Anything you silently fill in: unlabeled colors, code-name labels, a misplaced colorbar zero Ask a colleague A great deal Costs a colleague's time on every draft This tool: blind Claude review Whether the message survives in the pixels, plus jargon, rainbow colormaps, clipping, midpoint errors Whether the physics is right

Claude Code plugin (skills and reviewer together)

The reviewer starts with uv and uses either:

- Claude Code, installed and signed in (backend claudecli, no API key), or - ANTHROPICAPIKEY in your environment (backend anthropicapi).

- Two modes. daily (seaborn white and talk, for notes and notebooks) and paper (serif, Computer Modern math, grey boxed frame with inward major and minor ticks, capless error bars). - P
