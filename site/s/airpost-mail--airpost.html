About airpost

Airpost is a desktop email client for macOS and Linux, forked from Mailspring, with its sync engine rewritten in Rust. It works with Gmail, Microsoft 365, Outlook.com and any IMAP/SMTP server, and it needs no account or server of its own: download it, add a mail account, and your mail syncs directly between your computer and your provider. On top of the desktop app, the same engine can be driven from a terminal, by AI agents over MCP, and from a browser through a web client you host yourself.

- Mail, calendar and contacts. Unified inbox, threads, labels and folders, search with from:, in:, is:, has:attachment, before:/after: and boolean operators. Calendars and contacts sync over CalDAV and CardDAV (Google contacts through the People API). - Local features that need no server. Snooze, send later, send reminders, templates, mail rules, one-click unsubscribe, undo send, signatures. - Privacy defaults. Remote images are blocked until you load them, tracking pixels from known trackers are stripped from incoming mail, contact photos from Gravatar are off, and mail whose reply-to address differs from the sender's gets a phishing warning. - Rust sync engine (airpost-engine). One process holds all your accounts and serves the desktop, the CLI, the MCP server and the gateway over a local socket. It can keep running after the app quits, so scheduled sends still go out. - The airpost command. Read, search, draft and send mail; archive, move, label, snooze and unsubscribe; mail rules, templates, signatures, contacts, calendar, tasks, logs and a live watch, with --json on everything. I
