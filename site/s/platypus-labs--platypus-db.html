About platypus-db

A fast, small SQL client for people who work with many databases.

PlatypusDB™ is a desktop SQL client for developers, DBAs and analysts who work with several databases in a day. One small app runs queries, browses and edits data, designs tables, explains slow statements and documents schemas, and keeps production connections clearly apart from the others. A Rust core with a Tauri 2 + React interface: an installer of about 20 MB, a window ready in under a second, and a results grid that scrolls through millions of rows.

- Light. A 20 MB installer on Windows and a 30 MB download on Linux, against 122 MB for DBeaver, 346 MB for Beekeeper Studio and about 1 GB for DataGrip; a window in under a second. - Safe with production. Connection types color the window frame, risky statements ask first, production opens in manual commit, and every save of edited rows leaves an undo script. - Many databases, one app. PostgreSQL, MySQL, MariaDB, SQL Server, Oracle, ClickHouse, SQLite, DuckDB, MongoDB, Redis, Elasticsearch, Cassandra, Snowflake, BigQuery and more, with the same editor and grid. - Ready for agents. Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and other MCP clients use your saved connections with the access you give each one, and their changes wait for your OK. - Open source. AGPL-3.0, no account, no telemetry.

Download the installer for your system from the releases page: .exe/.msi for Windows, .dmg for macOS, .AppImage/.deb/.rpm for Linux. The app updates itself.
