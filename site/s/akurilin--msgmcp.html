About msgmcp

A local MCP server, written in Rust, for message history on a Mac. It reads iMessage and SMS from chat.db, WhatsApp Desktop from its ChatStorage.sqlite, Signal Desktop from its encrypted db.sqlite and Telegram for macOS from its encrypted Postbox cache, all read-only, looks people up in Contacts, WhatsApp's address book, Signal conversations and Telegram's contacts, and sends iMessage text through the Messages app.

Agents increasingly run in cloud sandboxes (Claude, Codex, Grok and others), but your iMessages, texts, WhatsApp, Signal and Telegram chats and contacts live on your Mac. msgmcp reads their local data; macOS protects some of it with Full Disk Access. An agent that could answer "what did Sarah say about Saturday?" or draft a reply has no way to see them.

The obvious ways to bridge that gap are unsafe:

- Uploading the databases hands over every conversation you've ever had, to keep somewhere you don't control. - Opening the Mac to the agent, through SSH or an exposed port, gives it far more than your messages, and anything that hijacks the agent gets the same. - Letting it send freely means a stranger's message, read by the agent, can steer it into texting anyone.

msgmcp gives a cloud agent a narrow door instead. One signed app on the Mac holds the permissions and offers a few tools: search, list and read conversations, look up contacts, and send an iMessage. Agents reach it only over your private Tailscale network, where they're tagged and limited to that one port, and every request needs a token.
