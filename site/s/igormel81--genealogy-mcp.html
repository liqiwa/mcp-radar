About genealogy-mcp

genealogy-mcp — find your family history with any AI assistant

An MCP server that turns Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or any MCP-capable assistant into a careful genealogist. It knows where the records of your ancestors are (114 archives and databases across Russia and the former USSR, Europe, the British Isles, the USA and Latin America), how to search them (name variants, transliterations, old spelling, the Julian calendar), and when a find really is your person — so the assistant gives you working links and a plan instead of confident guesses.

Built from years of real research for an open family archive powered by genearchive: parish registers, revision lists, WWI and WWII databases, repression files, emigration records — and every trap we fell into along the way. The story behind it: an article on Habr (in Russian).

- 114 archives and databases in 55 countries, each with what it holds and how to search it well — and which archive holds the records of a historical province today (borders moved; the answer is often not the obvious one). - 24 tools for the real work of a genealogist: name variants and 9 transliteration systems, Daitch–Mokotoff, Julian, Hebrew and French Republican calendars, dates by church feasts, Church Slavonic numerals, kinship in three languages, DNA, evidence checks, a tree audit, live search in open databases, archive request drafts. - It works on your tree, not beside it.
