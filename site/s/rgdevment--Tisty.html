About Tisty

Tisty — Shared Working Memory for You and Your AI Agent

You see the whole. Your agent, every detail. One shared memory.

Tisty is a desktop app where you plan your tasks in natural language and keep what you know: steps, linked documents, ideas and a diary. Brainsty draws it all joined, as one network.

You and your AI agent read and write in that same memory, on your disk, in Markdown. It works without an agent too, the traditional way: your notes, documents and tasks, by hand and at your own pace.

Free and open source, for Windows and macOS, with a local MCP server that lets GitHub Copilot, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or any agent pick up where it left off, a section at a time.No accounts. No subscriptions. No telemetry. No server.

Software out of conviction, not subscription.

Prefer a direct download? GitHub Releases carries the signed installers — Windows (.exe) · macOS (.dmg, one per chip)

I started building Tisty to solve a problem of my own, and little by little I saw it was not only mine: everyone ran into it, and each solved it their own way.

You stop explaining. It stops starting over. Your AI agent knows the details better than anyone, and forgets them the moment the session ends. Everybody patches that in their own way: a NOTES.md hidden behind .gitignore, a Markdown file per session, a TASKS.md nobody keeps up to date, an AGENTS.md that only grows. They go stale, they pile up, they get lost between branches, machines and projects — and the agent rereads all of it, every session.
