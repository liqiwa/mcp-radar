About cheatspecter

A real-time modding tool for jailbroken PS5 consoles that runs in a web browser. Load one small payload on the console, open a page on your phone or computer, and switch cheats on, or find and make your own.

Use at your own risk. CheatSpecter changes the memory of a running game, which can crash it or damage a save. It has only been tested on a Jailbroken 13.60 PS5. What you do with it is your responsibility.

You need a jailbroken PS5 that can load ELF payloads, with ps5debug-NG running, and any device with a browser on the same network. Prerequisites has the details.

1. Download cheatspecter-v.elf from the Releases page. 2. On the PS5, start ps5debug-NG (etaHEN can start it from its toolbox). 3. Send the ELF to the console the way you load any payload, for example by uploading it in a payload manager. 4. A notification on the TV shows an address. Open it in a browser on the same network: http://:8744 5. Start a game. Cheats made by the community for that game appear as switches on the Cheats tab. To make your own, press Guide me through my first cheat.

Your cheats are saved on the PS5 by themselves as you make them, and open from there the next time the game runs. Save downloads a copy to the device you browse from. (New in 0.4.3, beta.)

The payload has to be loaded again after each reboot, like any payload. Nothing is installed on the device you browse from. To stop it without a reboot, open the page from the PS5's address, press Settings (the gear at the top) and choose Shut down CheatSpecter on the PS5 (beta).
