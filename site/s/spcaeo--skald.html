About skald

Say it “skawld” (/skɔːld/), one syllable, rhymes with “called”: an old Norse poet who told what happened.

Every visit, truly told. Self-hosted web analytics that reads your server's own access log, so you see the people, the AI assistants, the crawlers and the bots that a JavaScript tracker never sees.

Live demo · Quick start · Screenshots · Comparison · Install guide · Security · FAQ · Hosted / commercial

Your analytics cannot see the new visitors

More and more people ask ChatGPT, Claude or Perplexity before they visit a website. When they do, the assistant fetches your pages to write its answer. AI companies also crawl your site to train their models. None of that runs JavaScript, so Google Analytics, Plausible and other script-based tools never count it. Google Analytics also drops known bots on purpose.

Skald reads the one record that sees every visit: your web server's access log. Every request lands in exactly one of five groups, and the five add up to the log exactly.

Group What is in it --- --- People A browser that is none of the groups below. Self-reported, so an upper bound. AI assistants Split by why they came: to train, because someone just asked a question, or a person who clicked a link in an AI answer. Search engines Google, Bing and other classic indexing. Bots and scanners Monitors, SEO tools, chat link previews, and probes hunting for .env or wp-login. Your own traffic Your team, your crawler, your tests. Kept apart, never counted as people.
