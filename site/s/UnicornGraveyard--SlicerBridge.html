About SlicerBridge

Any multicolor 3MF project in. A ready, checked file for every multicolor printer out.

You paint a multicolor model in one slicer. The people who print it own a Bambu Lab A1, a Creality K2, an Elegoo Centauri Carbon, a QIDI Q2, a Prusa XL, a Snapmaker U1… and each of them has to retarget your project to their printer by hand, guessing at presets, and often losing the colors on the way.

SlicerBridge takes the project from whichever slicer you made it in (Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, or a printer maker's own fork of either) and writes one file for each of 34 printers, named for that printer (My Model - Creality K2 Plus.3mf), so a downloader can open theirs and print. Its files for Bambu Lab, OrcaSlicer-family and U1 printers can go back in, for yet another printer. Every file:

- keeps the colors where you painted them. The filaments a model actually uses are kept in order, and every painted triangle is re-encoded to match. - carries that printer's own settings, written by its own slicer, and that printer's filament presets. Your print settings (layer height, walls, infill, supports) come along where the printer has the same setting. - keeps its size. Nothing is ever scaled. A model bigger than a bed gets a warning with the scale that fits. - is checked before it's written: colors, paint, plates, size and settings, and optionally sliced in that printer's slicer to prove automatic color changes and a prime tower clear of the model. A file that fails a check is never written.
