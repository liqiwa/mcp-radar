About agentring

Your AI agent can call you. When it needs a decision, hits a wall or finishes a long job, your phone rings like a real call, even locked and in Doze. A voice AI tells you what's going on, you talk it through, and the agent gets the transcript and carries on. You can also call your agents back from the app and ask them things by voice.

Self-hosted and open source. No phone numbers, no Twilio, no per-minute fees: calls go over VoIP from your own server to your own phone.

Pre-release. Messages, calls on a locked phone, the voice AI, calling your agent from the app and the MCP server all work. So far it has been tested on one phone (Pixel 6, Android 16). Android only for now.

On a call, a voice AI explains why the agent is calling, answers your questions and hands the transcript back. - You reach your agents. Tap "Call agent" in the app. The voice AI can ask your own agents for help mid-call.
