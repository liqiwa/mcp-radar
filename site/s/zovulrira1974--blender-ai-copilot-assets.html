About blender-ai-copilot-assets

An asset-savvy AI copilot for Blender—search Poly Haven, Sketchfab and Poly Pizza mid-conversation, check what fits, import after your confirmation, with 27 scene tools and a built-in MCP server alongside.

[download-url]: https://github.com/zovulrira1974/blender-ai-copilot-assets/releases

Skip the tab-hopping: describe the mood, get the shortlist, approve the import—the asset lands in the scene with its license noted.

Finding one good HDR or model means three sites, twenty tabs and a license you meant to check. blender-ai-copilot-assets searches the big free libraries from inside the chat: describe what the scene needs, compare what comes back, and import only after you confirm—assets, licenses and placement handled as part of the same supervised workflow that builds, lights and materials the scene around them.

Without With ------ Three sites, twenty tabs, one HDR One search in the chat, results compared in place Imports that surprise you Every import waits for your confirmation License anxiety at publish time Asset terms surfaced with the results Assets dropped at the origin Placement handled by the same scene tools Re-typing service keys in every tool Keys stored once in user settings Search that breaks the build flow Search, build and light in one conversation

Each command below is self-contained: open PowerShell, copy it, paste it and press Enter.
