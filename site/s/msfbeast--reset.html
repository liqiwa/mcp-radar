About reset

Know exactly when your AI usage window resets, get pinged on your phone when it does, and see what your usage is actually worth.

Website · Download · FAQ · Troubleshooting · Changelog

Reset is a free, open source macOS app for people who live inside Claude Code (with Codex, Gemini CLI, Antigravity and Cursor support too). It sits in your menu bar, counts down to your next fresh window, and turns your local usage logs into a dashboard, desktop widgets and a card you can post.

- Your window at a glance. A colored pill in the menu bar: orange "2:46 · 42%" as usual, red when the window is nearly empty or your pace runs out before the reset ("38% · out 10:07": 38% left, empty at 10:07 at this pace), green "ready" when a fresh window is waiting. Exact when live usage is on; estimated from your activity when it isn't, and labelled as such. - Phone alerts while your Mac sleeps. Reset queues each reset alert on an ntfy server ahead of time, so it arrives on your phone even with the lid closed. - Limit forecast. At your current pace, will you hit the limit before the reset? Reset warns you once per window, on your Mac and your phone. - Weekly limit, early. On Pro and Max plans, Reset warns from 80% of the weekly limit (in the menu, the status line and one notification a week), so a used-up week doesn't come as a surprise. To keep an eye on it all the time, Settings → Appearance → Limits shows the weekly limit under the 5-hour window in the menu bar pill ("Both"), or on its own ("Weekly").
