About job-opportunity-tracker

Keep your whole job search in one place: every opening, every application, every interview, and every person helping you along the way. Ask Claude where things stand.

About this repo: it's a public copy of the private repository I build and run my own tracker from. I keep working on the app there and copy it here periodically, so new features arrive in batches, each as a single commit, rather than as day-to-day history. Issues and suggestions are welcome; changes from pull requests are carried over by hand into the private repo and come back in a later update.

What changed in each update is in CHANGELOG.md and on the Releases page. The coverage and mutation-score badges show the numbers from the latest update.

Screenshots use made-up companies and people.

Looking for a job is a job. Whether you were laid off and need the next role soon, or you're employed and quietly looking for something better, the search gets messy fast:

- Twenty open tabs, a spreadsheet you stopped updating, and a pile of "thanks for applying" emails. - A recruiter calls back and you can't remember which role it was, what the salary range was, or when you applied. - A posting you meant to apply to closed last Tuesday. - A friend introduced you to someone useful three weeks ago, and you've lost track of what you talked about.

This app gives that chaos a shape. Each opportunity moves across a board, from Considering to Applied, Contacted, Interviewing and Offer, and every step is dated and kept. You can see at a glance what needs a follow-up, what's closing soon, and what pays enough to be worth you
