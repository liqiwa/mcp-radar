About AunoStudio

Auno Studio AI-native content creation, motion design, and social publishing in one self-hosted workspace. Turn ideas, documents, links, images, and footage into editable videos and channel-ready content.

Releases · Documentation · Self-hosting · Issues

Auno Studio brings AI-assisted creation into a native editing workflow. Generated content remains editable, so a first draft can become a finished production without leaving the workspace.

Capability What it does --------- Auno Auto Video Builds an editable video project from a prompt, document, URL, media selection, or structured idea. Documentary & Vox Style Turns research into ideas, scripts, timed beats, evidence-aware visuals, voice chunks, and motion compositions. Auno Motion Engine Applies reusable motion language and deterministic compositions while keeping timing and creative controls editable. Photo & Video Editors Combines layers, multitrack timelines, captions, transitions, effects, audio, and export tools. Publishing Adapts, previews, schedules, and publishes content across connected social channels. Connected Workspace Keeps media, accounts, calendars, inboxes, analytics, automations, and collaborators together.

AI output should be a starting point, not a locked render. Auno Studio creates native project state that can be refined in the editor: rearrange scenes, rewrite copy, retime voice, replace media, change motion, and export again.
