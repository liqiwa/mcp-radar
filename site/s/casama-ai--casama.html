About casama

Casama™: AI bots with their own computers. Bots that remember, reach out first, work on real computers and ask before risky steps.

casama.ai · Open the app · Docs · Gallery · Discord

Try it: open the web app, or get the app for your phone, computer or watch.

Casama is open source. If it's useful to you, a star on GitHub helps other people find it.

Casama gives you AI bots that each have their own computer. A bot remembers how you work, browses, fills in forms, writes files and runs code on a sandboxed machine you can watch and take over. It starts work on schedules and triggers, so it keeps going after you close the chat. It asks before anything risky.

You use Casama on your phone, on desktop (Mac, Windows and Linux), on the web, on your watch, and from the command line.

Casama is open source. The apps, the safety rules and the developer kit in this repo are open. The Casama Engine behind Casama Cloud is not. LICENSING.md says exactly what is open.
