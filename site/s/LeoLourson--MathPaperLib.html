About MathPaperLib

MathPaperLib keeps the papers you work with in one folder, ~/Literature, under names such as Bondarenko 2021 - Inverse spectral problems for functional-differential operators with involution.pdf, and gives an AI agent tools to find papers, download them, read them page by page and search their full texts. The tools run as an MCP server, which any agent harness with MCP support can start, and as the lit command.

arXiv and Math-Net.Ru serve files under identifiers (2107.12209v1.pdf, rm9740.pdf), so a folder of downloads cannot be read by eye. A PDF saved by hand from a publisher's site keeps the name the site gave it, and the tool that fetched the other papers knows nothing about it. When every project keeps its own papers, a paper used by two projects is downloaded and indexed twice.

MathPaperLib keeps one library per user. Each paper is stored once, under a name built from its authors, year and title. A PDF downloaded by hand goes into the inbox folder, where it is identified from its first pages and filed under the same rule. A project keeps only its registry, the list of papers it uses with their roles, and reads the files from the library. Full-text search covers LaTeX sources and the text layers of PDFs; it combines BM25 with the multilingual embedding model intfloat/multilingual-e5-small, run on the CPU.

library.json records the metadata of every paper, its identifiers (arXiv, DOI, Math-Net.Ru), the names of its files and their SHA-256 checksums. .lit/ holds what is derived from the papers: text layers of PDFs, rendered pages, parts of transcriptions and the search
