About sushruta-anatomy

A local-first 3D anatomy atlas for medical students.

Built by Dr. Atul Tiwari at Vedant Research Labs. Named after the Sushruta Samhita, the ~6th century BCE surgical compendium.

An interactive atlas over 3,534 anatomical structures, with theory written for people sitting exams, and a study assistant that drives the 3D view as it answers.

Nothing leaves the device. Not the questions you ask, not the notes you import, not your revision history. That is the design, not a setting you have to find.

- Explore — the specimen, with ghosting, x-ray, isolate, explode, scan, tissue colour, a depth probe, lasso select, and landmarks. - The assistant — docks beside the stage and moves it. "X-ray the bone", "highlight the axis", "trace from the atlas to the vertebra C6" are answered with no model configured and nothing on the network, by a deterministic router. A local server or a cloud key unlocks prose on top. - Dissect · Present · Systems · Review · Practical · Lessons · Notes — layered removal, a presenter mode driven from across the room, spaced repetition, a spotter exam, guided walkthroughs, and local PDF search. - An MCP server — Claude calls the atlas directly and renders the same viewer inside the conversation, over the MCP Apps handshake.
