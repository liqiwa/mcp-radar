About vocal-teleprompter

A teleprompter that scrolls with your voice.

The script moves as you speak and waits when you pause. Keep it over your slides or right under your webcam, and hide it from screen sharing if you want. Free, open source, and it runs offline, on Windows and Linux.

No fixed speed, no foot pedal. Speech recognition finds where you are in the script and scrolls there, word by word. Pause, slow down or ad-lib, and it waits for you; skip a sentence and it catches up, while a stray word that also comes later never sends it jumping ahead. Works with any microphone, in 9 languages, entirely on your computer, with a small offline speech model that leaves room for your call.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/81646d99-4438-4440-b3b0-1bccc8ea6e0a

Put it wherever there is room on your slides, over photos, colors or text. What shows through the window is frosted like glass, live, so a video or a window you drag stays soft behind the text, and every letter takes the color that stands out most from what is right behind it. You never pick a color or hunt for an empty corner.

Dragged across photos, the glass follows live

Share your screen and only your slides go out. On Windows, Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, OBS, Discord and screenshots leave the prompter out, while you keep reading from it, right under your webcam. On Linux, Wayland lets only the desktop hide a window, so on GNOME the prompter's own Shell extension does it (see Download).
