About AssetForge

AssetForge — AI Game Asset Pipeline for Unreal Engine 5

Describe it once. Get a game-ready model, textures, sounds, VFX and synchronized animation systems imported straight into Unreal Engine.

AssetForge is a free and open-source desktop app for Windows that turns a short text brief into production-ready game assets for Unreal Engine 5. It chains AI services into one node-based pipeline:

Step Service Output --------- Prompt engine, art direction, vision QA Claude, or any OpenAI-compatible / local LLM (Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM, OpenRouter) Service-specific prompts that respect your project's style bible Text / image to 3D, retexture, rigging Meshy GLB/FBX meshes, PBR textures, UE5 Manny-compatible rigs Text to motion NVIDIA Kimodo (local, CUDA) Looping, root-motion animation clips Sound effects and dynamic engine audio ElevenLabs SFX plus RPM layers wired into a MetaSound Textures and concept images ComfyUI (local, any workflow) Tileable PBR textures and sprites Cleanup and LODs Blender (headless) Scaled, decimated and renamed FBX with LOD 50/25/10 % Import Unreal Engine 5 Python Remote Execution Assets in the right folders, material instances, Blend Spaces, Montages, Pose Search databases

It is built for indie developers, technical artists and students who want consistent, project-specific assets instead of one-off generations.
