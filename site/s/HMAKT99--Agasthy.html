About Agasthy

One intelligence layer for your developer machine.

Every repo, runtime, package and AI tool on your machine, connected in a live knowledge graph. Track coding agents and what they cost, see what breaks before you remove anything, free disk space safely, and ask plain questions about your machine with evidence behind every answer.

⬇ Download for Mac (Apple silicon) · ⬇ Windows preview · ⬇ Linux preview · free and open source · no account · no telemetry

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2718c8ae-1576-4c4f-a494-646731a019fa

A developer machine holds hundreds of moving parts: cloned repositories, several copies of the same runtime, packages from six package managers, AI tools with their own configs, and now coding agents starting servers in every terminal. Nothing shows you how they connect, what they cost, or what is safe to remove. AGASTHY reads the machine itself, links everything in one graph, and lets you act on it safely.

------ 📦 Everything, discovered Repositories (branch, uncommitted work, remotes, duplicate clones), applications, CLI tools and runtimes (which copy is active, which are shadowed), and packages from Homebrew, npm, pnpm, uv, pip and cargo. Every fact carries its evidence. 🕸️ A knowledge graph of your machine Projects, runtimes, dependencies, packages, tools and AI configuration, linked. See which projects share a dependency and what relies on what. 💥 Impact analysis "What breaks if I remove this?" for any package, runtime or tool, simulated before you touch anything. Try dependency upgrades in an isolated Update Lab. 🤖 Coding agents, live Ev
