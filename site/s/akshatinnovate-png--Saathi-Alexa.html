About Saathi-Alexa

Saathi (साथी) - a companion on Alexa+ for elders, and the families who love them

Saathi means "companion" in Hindi and Marathi.

Kamala is 78. Everyone calls her Aaji. She lives alone in Pune, speaks Marathi, has early-stage Alzheimer's and doesn't hear as well as she used to. Her granddaughter Riya is in Toronto and grew up speaking English. They love each other, but they share less and less language, and Riya worries every day she doesn't hear from her.

Where What to do ------ Windows Download Saathi (Source code (zip)), unzip it and double-click Start Saathi. It installs what it needs, asks a few questions once, and opens in your browser. Mac or Linux Download and unzip the same file, then run ./start.sh in that folder. Online, for the whole family Saathi gets its own web address that everyone can open, including the tablet by your elder's chair. Docker docker run -p 8787:8787 -v saathi-data:/data ghcr.io/akshatinnovate-png/saathi-alexa

Then open Saathi, tap Continue with Google (or use an email and password), and answer four short steps about the person you care for. On their tablet or Echo Show, open /device.html and type the six-digit code from the family app, then tap Approve when the family app shows the same three pictures as the device. That's all.

Deploying to Render uses its paid Starter plan, because Saathi keeps your family's data on a small disk and Render's free plan erases disks on every restart. Add SAATHIGOOGLECLIENTID and SAATHIGOOGLECLIENTSECRET in Render to switch on Google sign-in, with https://.onrender.com/auth/google/callback as the redirect ad
