About nextime

A self-hosted calendar for families and small groups: your own calendars on your own server, in the browser and, later, on iPhone and Android through CalDAV.

Early days. Everything below is built and in daily use; this is an early release, so keep a backup and open an issue when something is wrong. Native apps for iPhone and iPad come later; until then phones connect through CalDAV.

Today, across every calendar: what comes next and how soon, the rest of the day, and the next events of the days after. Quick entry at the top understands a line like "Tomorrow 3 pm dentist". On the left the views, own calendars, calendars shared with you, and public holidays in the background.

The week, with an event from a calendar shared by someone else carrying their initial. Events move by dragging; the column "Coming up" on the right folds away.

The month. Built-in public holidays stand out, and an event over several days shows on each of them.

An event opens over the page: times and time zone, busy or free, calendar and repeat, reminders that are yours alone, a place with a link to a map, notes with links.

Share a calendar with members of the server, each with a right of their own, or with guests through a read link they can subscribe to.

A device gets its own password: the iPhone scans a QR code and loads a profile, Android and Thunderbird take the address. On a phone the week shows three days, and nextime installs from the browser as an app.
