About content-factory

Open-source AI faceless video generator for TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. A free, local alternative to Higgsfield Content Factory: write a What if script, or let Claude write it, and get a finished vertical video with AI frames, motion, voiceover, word-by-word subtitles and music, made on your own Mac. Free and open source: no subscription, no credits, no watermark. By Jutsu Studio, open-source Mac tools for AI video.

Quick start &nbsp;·&nbsp; How it works &nbsp;·&nbsp; vs Higgsfield &nbsp;·&nbsp; vs MoneyPrinterTurbo &nbsp;·&nbsp; Claude and MCP &nbsp;·&nbsp; FAQ &nbsp;·&nbsp; Architecture &nbsp;·&nbsp; Contributing

What if every human vanished today? 57 s · AI frames · Low-poly City8 beats, a source for every fact. Script

What if the Sun went out? 31 s · Drawn · gouacheEvery scene is SVG code, no image generated. Script

What if you fell into Jupiter? 53 s · AI frames · Space EpicCuts between a wide shot and punch-ins on the diver. Script

Claude wrote each script and handed it to the engine over MCP; the scripts are in formats/what-if/examples/. The engine did the rest: frames, depth parallax, transitions, narration, word-by-word subtitles, CC0 music ducked under the voice, and a quality check.

Faceless short-video channels run on formats: the same structure, a new topic every day. Content Factory turns a format into a pipeline you own.

- No video model. Motion comes from a handful of AI stills: depth parallax, frame interpolation, layers and camera moves. A one-minute video needs only eight images. - Formats are data. A format is a folder with a scrip
