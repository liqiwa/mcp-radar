About apkscope

Open any Android app and see what it really does. What it collects, how it makes money from you, what it exposes. In seconds, as readable Java. Viewer, CLI and MCP server for AI agents. One executable. No Java needed.

Each one needed the manifest, the resources and the decompiled code read together. A grep for http:// finds none of it. Click a finding for the proof: real output, trimmed to the lines that matter.

1. Block Blast prices you for advertisers · 13 ad networks, and its own model of what you're worth

Block Blast! 10.9.3 (Play build), 75,938 classes, scanned in 3.1 s.

Who gets your data: AppLovin MAX, AdMob, Meta, Unity, ironSource, Pangle, Liftoff/Vungle, InMobi, Moloco, Amazon APS, Digital Turbine/Fyber, PubMatic, Bigo. Trackers: AppsFlyer, Firebase, Facebook and TikTok events, ThinkingData, the studio's own cn.hsdata. Permissions: advertising ID and all four Privacy Sandbox ad APIs.

Two endpoints aren't from any SDK. infer is a model call: what are this player's ads worth?

Those fill in deviceModel, ram, disk, cpu, carrier, country, ipcountry, timeZone, installtime, coldstartnum and your past ads with their revenue. The answer sets your floor price, the lowest bid an advertiser must beat to show you an ad:

Verdict: legal, and how ad-funded games work. Now you can see it.
