About Ledger

A self-hosted personal finance dashboard that pulls in your bank data on its own.

- Why I made it - What it does - Screenshots - Getting started - Self-hosting - Connecting accounts - Security - Configuration - Built with

I wanted one place to see my transactions, how much I'm earning, what subscriptions I'm paying for and how close I am to my goals, without logging into a bunch of different sites and copying numbers into a spreadsheet. So I built Ledger. You link your accounts once, and it signs in and fetches new transactions by itself in the background, so everything stays up to date without you touching it.

- Dashboard: net worth, cash flow and spending for the month, budgets, what needs attention, and quick links to connect a bank or upload statements. - Accounts & transactions: every linked account with balances, overdraft and credit limits, balance history, cash flow, upcoming payments and recent activity; a full Transactions page to search, filter, categorise and tag entries. - Insights: spending by category, top merchants and month-by-month cash flow. Transfers between your own accounts are ignored so nothing gets counted twice. Drag across a chart to zoom into a date range, then hit Reset zoom. - Budgets: set monthly limits by category on the dashboard and see when you're over. - Subscriptions: track recurring payments on any schedule, see them on a calendar, and get suggestions for recurring charges it spots in your transactions. - Goals: save up for something or pay something off.
