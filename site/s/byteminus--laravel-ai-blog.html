About laravel-ai-blog

Laravel AI Blog — the AI-native blog package for Laravel

Website and docs: byteminus.github.io/laravel-ai-blog · For AI assistants: llms.txt

Install it, and your blog thinks. Laravel AI Blog is a free, open-source blog package for Laravel 10 to 13. It plans topics, drafts with you, scores every post for on-page SEO, links itself, keeps itself fresh — and lets Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code or any MCP agent write to it.

- Headless by default. Models, migrations, a trait for your existing Post model. No admin panel forced on you. Flip one flag for /blog routes, Blade components, RSS and JSON-LD. - SEO that nobody else ships. An on-page score with plain-English fixes, internal-link suggestions with anchor text, stale-post detection, BlogPosting + FAQPage schema. - AI as your editor, not your ghostwriter. Built on the official laravel/ai SDK — any provider. Brief → draft, meta descriptions, rewrites, refresh suggestions, translations. Everything lands as a draft. Nothing auto-publishes unless you say so. - Agent-ready. A built-in laravel/mcp server: "write a post on X, score it, link it to related posts, schedule for Monday." - Free. MIT. No Nova license, no Filament lock-in, no SaaS tether.

- PHP 8.2 to 8.5 with the curl, dom, libxml and mbstring extensions - Laravel 10, 11, 12 or 13

Both ends of those ranges run in CI on every push: the newest dependencies and the oldest ones the constraints allow. Laravel 10 and 11 are end of life; they are supported for apps that have not upgraded, not recommended for new ones. On PHP 8.4 and later the text extractor uses PHP's spec-comp
