About kufar-mcp

Talk to Kufar.by through Claude or any other MCP client. Search the catalog in plain language, see how your own listings are doing, keep an eye on saved searches and get through your Kufar inbox without clicking through the site.

Unofficial project, not affiliated with Kufar. It talks to the same web API the site uses, so it can break when Kufar changes it. Kufar's terms don't welcome automation: this is built for one person managing their own account, not for scraping. See Legal.

Finding things faster. Describe what you want the way you'd tell a friend. The model turns it into Kufar's filters (category, region, Minsk district, price range in BYN, condition, private or company seller, photo only, Kufar delivery, exchange), reads the results and the ad details, and can compare a dozen ads in one answer.

Watching the market. Ask what's new for a query since this morning, or keep saved searches on your account and ask which ones have new ads. Handy for rare items and for checking prices before you list something yourself.

Knowing how your listings perform. Views, phone reveals, favorites, publish and expiry dates, days left and what Kufar lets you do with each ad (prolong, activate, delete), for every tab of "Мои объявления". "Which listing gets views but no calls?" becomes a one-line question.

Handling messages. See unread chats, read a conversation with the ad it's about, and draft a reply. The model shows you the text first and sends it only after you approve it.
