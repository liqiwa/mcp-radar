About 37studio

An AI video studio and social media desk that runs on your own computer. Videos made, posted and answered, with your own keys and no monthly bill.

▶ Watch the intro video · Setup guide · Issues · Donate

37Studio makes videos for a business and runs its social accounts, from one app on your own computer. Add a company from its website and it builds a brand library and a knowledge base. Brief a video and Claude Code, the editor agent, builds it as a HyperFrames composition in that brand, checks its own frames and renders it on your machine. Publish it to the company's accounts with a caption written for each platform, now, on a schedule or on Autopilot. Messages and comments land in one inbox with replies drafted from the knowledge base.

You bring your own keys (a Claude plan or an Anthropic API key; Gemini and ElevenLabs optional) and pay the providers directly. Every project shows what it cost in AI usage, in USD by default. 37Studio is free and open source under the MIT License, made by Shaitrish, a software team in Dhaka.

- Brand library from a website or folder. Paste a URL, or pick the company's own project folder, and get its logos, people, photos, fonts and exact colours sorted and captioned, plus a design system (design.md) and a knowledge base (knowledge.md). - An editor agent. Claude Opus 5.5 builds each video as an animated HyperFrames composition, checks its own frames and takes changes in plain words. Edit by hand on a real timeline when you want to. - Many kinds of output. Hook reels, captioned clips, motion-graphics explainers, podcast clips, promo ads, sq
