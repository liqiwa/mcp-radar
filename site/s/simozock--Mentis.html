About Mentis

Mentis is a free and open-source desktop application for students: mind maps, Gantt plans, notes and presentations in one place, with images, attachments, links and SVG stickers, so that everyone can study efficiently and effectively — whatever computer they have.

Mentis is developed in Switzerland and in Europe, and it respects European privacy rules: it follows the GDPR and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP / LPD).

Mentis is Latin for "of the mind". It also echoes the word mantis, which inspired our mascot. The mantis is a patient and attentive observer: it takes in its surroundings, remains focused and acts with precision. These are also valuable qualities when creating a mind map — taking the time to explore an idea, identifying the connections that matter and choosing the few key words that make the map truly meaningful to its creator.

The icon shows the mantis inside its glass tile, while the smiling character beside it is the mascot — a friendly companion for giving ideas shape and making complex thoughts clearer.

Mind maps - An infinite canvas with zoom, pan, multiple selection and an automatically balanced layout. - Many map structures: mind map, logic chart, org chart, tree, tree table, brace map, timeline, fishbone and more, each with ready-made style variants. - Topic styles: shapes, colours, colour palettes by level, branch styles, numbering, fonts, and copy/paste of a style. - Relationships between topics, boundaries, summaries and floating topics. - Several sheets in one document; move a branch to a new sheet.
