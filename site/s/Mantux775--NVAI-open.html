About NVAI-open

Local image, audio, video and 3D generation on your own computer. No cloud, no subscription, no account, no content filter.

------ Windows 10/11, NVIDIA or AMD Radeon NV-AI-Setup-0.5.3.exe macOS 15+ on Apple Silicon, signed and notarized NV-AI-0.4.2.dmg

All releases: github.com/Mantux775/NVAI-open/releases

October 2026: NV-AI 0.5.3 for Windows (release notes); the Mac app stays at 0.4.2

------ LoRAs Search Hugging Face and Civitai, NV-AI checks which model a LoRA fits before downloading; pick LoRAs with a strength slider when generating, images and video New video models LTX-2.3 with sound (101 s for 4 s at 1280 × 704), Mochi 1 (Apache-2.0), Kandinsky 5.0 Video Lite (MIT) Expand prompt everywhere Now also for video (motion, camera, sound), sound effects, music tags and 3D ("object from text") Image to video with LTX The start image picker now also shows up for LTX-2.5 and LTX-2.3

Since 0.5.2: LTX-2.5 with sound, Boogu-Image, HiDream-O1, censored/uncensored labels, cancel, storage locations. Since 0.5.0/0.5.1: report a problem as an anonymized GitHub issue, AMD Radeon and several graphics cards (experimental, testers wanted: 1, 2), expand prompt on the Mac.
