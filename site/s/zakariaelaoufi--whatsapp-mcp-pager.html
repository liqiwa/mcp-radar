About whatsapp-mcp-pager

Walk away from your desk. Control your AI coding agent from WhatsApp.

whatsapp-mcp-pager is a Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) MCP server for AI coding agents (Antigravity, Cursor, Claude Code, Windsurf).

When your agent wants to run a risky command or needs your input, it pauses and pings you on WhatsApp. You can reply with a quick text (Y / N) or send a voice note while on the go.

1. Agent Pauses: Your AI hits a risky task (e.g., database migration, deploy, rm -rf) or has a question. 2. WhatsApp Ping: It sends you a formatted text message + a spoken voice note explaining what it needs. 3. Wake-Up Ring: If you don't reply within 60 seconds, it rings your WhatsApp for 6 seconds to grab your attention. 4. You Decide: Reply with text (Y, N, or new instructions) or record a voice note. Your agent transcribes it and continues.

You need two WhatsApp accounts (two phone numbers):

1. Agent Number (Bot): A spare phone number or secondary WhatsApp account. You will link this account via QR code (npm run login). The AI uses this number to message and call you. 2. Your Personal Number: Your main WhatsApp on your phone (MYWHATSAPPID). This is where you receive alerts and reply to your agent.

You also need: - Node.js 20+ - OpenAI API Key (optional — enables spoken Opus voice notes and Whisper STT; without it, the pager runs in text-only mode) - Groq API Key (optional — enables ultra-fast Whisper voice-note transcription)
