About whatsapp-readonly-mcp

🔒 Secure&nbsp;&nbsp;·&nbsp;&nbsp;🛡️ Private&nbsp;&nbsp;·&nbsp;&nbsp;✋ Never sends by itself

The secure, private WhatsApp MCP: your AI talks straight to your own WhatsApp Web. No relay, no servers in between, never sends a message by itself.

WhatsApp Readonly MCP is a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server plus a Chrome extension for WhatsApp Web. It reads your chats and saves reply drafts, and never sends a message by itself. The Chrome extension reads the WhatsApp Web tab you already have open. The local MCP server gives that data to your AI app on the same computer. Nothing goes through our servers. We run none.

In the stores the product is called AI for Business Chat Assistant, by AI for Business.

Unofficial. Not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by WhatsApp or Meta. "WhatsApp" is used only to say which website this works with. WhatsApp's terms limit automated access, so try it with a spare or work number first.

Compatibility · Install · Tools · Secure & private · FAQ · Privacy · Security policy · Disclaimer · Development

For people who use AI every day and often need its help with WhatsApp: who is waiting for a reply, what a busy group said, a first draft of an answer. It is a convenience, not a way around WhatsApp.

- Fast and light on tokens. It hands your AI just the chats and messages it asked for, instead of making it read the whole web page. In our benchmark it answered 2.4–2.8× faster and used 3.4–5.3× fewer tokens than AI reading WhatsApp Web through a browser (Playwright MCP or Claude in Chrome). - Never sends a message by itself.
