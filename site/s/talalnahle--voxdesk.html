About voxdesk

An AI voice assistant that answers and places your phone calls from a Windows PC, using your own ElevenLabs voice agent.

VoxDesk sits in the Windows tray. When a WhatsApp call (or a cellular call through Microsoft Phone Link) comes in, it picks up, and an ElevenLabs conversational agent talks to the caller: it greets them, takes a message, follows the language they speak, says goodbye and hangs up. You get the summary and the full transcript afterwards. You can also ask it to call someone with a task ("confirm tomorrow's 1 pm meeting"), from the app or from an AI assistant such as Claude, Gemini, Antigravity or ChatGPT through MCP.

Dashboard · a call transcript · settings (demo data)

It is an open-source experiment that shows what can be built by joining a voice agent, desktop automation and a virtual audio cable. Read the disclaimer before you use it.

- Answers incoming calls on WhatsApp Desktop and on Phone Link (your phone's SIM line), for everyone, only your contacts, or nobody. - Places calls with a task you type, on WhatsApp. Cellular outbound is experimental (see Known limitations). - Speaks in any voice in your ElevenLabs account, including your own cloned voice, and switches language with the caller. - Neutral, honest default prompt. The assistant says it is an AI assistant when asked, and never invents facts or promises. - Short calls that end themselves.
