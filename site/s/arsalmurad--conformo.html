About conformo

Free, open-source tools to create and check e-invoices for Germany, France and the rest of the EU: XRechnung, ZUGFeRD, Factur-X, UBL and Peppol BIS. Everything runs on your own computer or in your browser. No Java, no server, no account, and your invoice data never goes to us or anyone else.

The web app, unedited: a typo in the authority's Leitweg-ID is caught as you type, the official XRechnung rules accept the finished invoice, and the PDF with the XML inside downloads. Recorded by tools/readme-media.mjs.

Proof, not promises. Conformo checks invoices with the official rule files, unmodified, and agrees 100% with the official KoSIT validator on the official test invoices. CI measures this on every change (conformance report). A daily job watches KoSIT, CEN and OpenPeppol for new rule releases, and a new version only ships if agreement is still 100% (how updates work).

You are Start here ------ A freelancer or small business that has to send e-invoices Make one in your browser. Free, no signup, nothing uploaded. Someone who received an e-invoice or wants to test one Check it in your browser. Drop the PDF or XML, see what to fix. A developer adding e-invoices to your software npm install @conformo/toolkit, then the Quickstart below or the developer docs. Building with AI agents (Claude, Cursor and others) The MCP server lets an agent create and check e-invoices locally.

Why now: German businesses must be able to receive e-invoices since 2025 and must send them from 2027 (turnover above EUR 800,000) or 2028 (everyone else). France started in September 2026. Short version
