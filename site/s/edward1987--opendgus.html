About opendgus

The open-source designer for DWIN T5L (DGUS II) touch displays, with an AI designer built in.

Download · Features · AI designer · Your microcontroller · Build from source

DWIN's T5L displays are cheap, bright and capable, but the official DGUS tool is Windows-only, closed, and hard to learn. Many of these displays end up in a drawer. OpenDGUS replaces the whole workflow: design the screens visually (or ask an AI to), try them in an emulator, and flash them to the display over USB, all in one app.

------ 🎨 Visual editor Drag-and-drop pages, text, images and controls. Save a selection as a reusable project preset. New live text, clock, QR, choice and curve controls expose more DGUS features without raw records. ✨ AI designer Describe the screen and the AI builds it, sees its own renders, and fixes what looks wrong. Works with Claude, OpenRouter, any OpenAI-compatible API, or local Ollama. ▶️ Emulator Click through your UI on the desktop, change variables like your MCU would, and watch the UART frames it sends, before you flash anything. ⚡ One-click upload Flash over the USB/UART adapter with a live progress bar, or export to a microSD card. Uses the exact timing of DWIN's own downloader. 🖥️ Any display 26 built-in DWIN models, part-number decoding (DMG10600C07003WTC → 1024×600, 7", capacitive, 16 MB), or any custom resolution, in landscape or portrait. 📐 22 templates Responsive starting points that adapt to any resolution, including a universal control panel and status dashboard (see below). 🔌 Controller code Generates Arduino, MicroPython and Python controller programs.
