About calends

The settlement day for everything you subscribe to. A self-hosted, AI-native subscription ledger — multi-currency, shared plans, prepaid balances, reminders, and a built-in MCP server.

Website · Live demo · Quick start · AI &amp; MCP · 简体中文

Track what you pay, when it renews, and who owes you. Calends brings subscriptions, shared plans and prepaid balances into one private, single-owner ledger, with an optional read-only public page and AI access through MCP.

- Flexible billing — monthly, yearly, custom cycles and lifetime purchases, with trials, intro prices, bonus periods, pauses, advance charging and cancellation deadlines. - A history that adds up — keep price, plan and region changes, gaps and overlapping subscriptions. See total spent, actual monthly cost and every charge. - Multiple currencies — historical exchange rates for past charges, current rates for forecasts, and your choice of display currency. - Balances and shared plans — track prepaid credit and gift cards, estimate when balances run out, and manage payments for friends. Shared plans have dated collection rules, member overrides, independent coverage periods and overdue tracking; people have private avatars and a separate account for each plan. - Reports and calendar — spending trends, cash vs. accrual, prepaid value, cancellation savings, subscription checkups and a shareable year in review. Includes month/year calendars and a private ICS feed. - Renewal reminders — email, Telegram, Lark/Feishu, DingTalk, WeCom, Slack, Discord, Bark, ntfy, ServerChan and custom webhooks. - AI access — query and update
