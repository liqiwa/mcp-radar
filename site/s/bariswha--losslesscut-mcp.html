About losslesscut-mcp

Let Claude and other AI assistants see your videos and cut them with LosslessCut.

An MCP server that looks at your media with FFmpeg (frames, scenes, silence, loudness, keyframes) and drives LosslessCut through its local HTTP API. Ask "keep the colour bars and the last 3 seconds and export them" and get the files, cut losslessly.

Real run against LosslessCut 3.70.2: every step is an MCP tool call, the screenshots are the LosslessCut window.

These run locally with FFmpeg and work on any file, with or without LosslessCut open. They mirror what LosslessCut itself can show or detect.

Tool What it does --- --- mediainfo Duration, container, streams (codec, resolution, fps, audio channels, language) and chapters contactsheet One image with a grid of frames, to scan a whole video or a part of it viewframes Frames at exact times, or evenly spaced, as images the assistant can look at detectscenes Scene changes and hard cuts detectsilence Silent parts, and the parts with sound between them detectblack Black parts, such as fades and breaks audiolevels Loudness over time (EBU R128) and the loudest moments keyframes Keyframes, and the ones around given times, to see where a lossless cut will land checktools Shows which FFmpeg and FFprobe it uses
