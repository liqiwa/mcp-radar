About yantra

Make your AI show its work. Diagrams that explain, one step at a time. Don't draw it. Explain it.

Website · Docs · Playground · Examples · Changelog

Diagrams take hours to draw and are out of date by Friday. Ask an AI instead and you get a wall of text, or a diagram where boxes overlap and arrows point nowhere.

Yantra is an open-source explanation engine. Describe a system, a process or a decision, or ask your coding agent or AI chat to. Yantra lays it out, checks it, and turns it into a walkthrough anyone can step through: a diagram, an answer page or a book, ready to share.

1. Describe it. Write a few lines of JSON, or ask Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Antigravity or any AI chat to write them. There are no boxes to place: layout, edge routing and label placement are automatic. 2. Yantra checks it. The validator catches broken references, overlapping cards, edges through cards and colliding labels before anyone sees them. Every finding has a stable code, so an agent can fix its own mistakes. 3. Readers step through it. Each step lights up the cards and edges that matter, with a short explanation, so anyone can follow a request, a process or a decision. 4. Share it anywhere. You get one HTML file that makes no network requests: email it, attach it to a ticket or open it offline. SVG, PNG and Markdown exports are there too.

The same format makes diagrams of every kind, answer pages with charts, books read like an e-reader, and slides. Bring your Mermaid, PlantUML, draw.io or Visio diagrams with you.
