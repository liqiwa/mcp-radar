About universal-animator

Universal Animator Make any animation fit any humanoid model.A desktop lab that plays an animation on the character it was made for and on your model, side by side, scores the fit, shows exactly where the body strains, and fixes it with you or with your AI.

Vibecoded. This whole project was made by prompting an AI (Claude Code): the app, the neural network code, the installer, the packaging, and even this GitHub push. Expect rough edges. It is for non-commercial use.

Grab the latest build from the Releases page:

Platform File ------ Windows UniversalAnimationLab-Setup.exe (installs for your account, no admin needed) Linux UniversalAnimationLab-x8664.AppImage (chmod +x, then run)

The Windows installer was cross-compiled and has had limited testing on real Windows machines. Please open an issue if something breaks.

See it - Original character and your model play the same clip side by side - Skeleton, X-ray, skinned-mesh and skin-weight heat map views - Deformation view: colours the body by where it stretches, squashes or glitches - Gap view: colours each body region by its distance from the original character - Orbit camera and a free-fly camera (drag to look, middle-drag to pan, scroll to move, WASD and Q/E to fly, Shift for speed) - Big side toolbar for every view, plus a reset-view button

Judge it - Scores 1 to 10 stars for Viability, Aesthetic, Human-like, Flexibility, Stability, Function and Body - Clear PASS or FAIL with the weakest category named, the worst moments, and the worst body part - A built-in pose network (trained on about 392,000 poses) that knows what
