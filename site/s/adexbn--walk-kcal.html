About walk-kcal

English TL;DR (the README below is in Chinese)

Walk-Kcal turns walking distance into a meal calorie budget: 450 kcal + 4 kcal per 80 m. Walking 800 m buys you +44 kcal — about half an apple.

Given a location, it lists nearby restaurants and their recommended dishes, estimates kcal per dish in explicit layers (exact recipe → category median → store-type prior → "unknown"), and marks each dish as fits your budget or over. McDonald's nutrition comes from the official China MCP (query-nearby-stores, list-nutrition-foods); other restaurants are estimated from a Chinese recipe corpus, with the layer and error range disclosed per dish.

⚠️ Not a turnkey tool for your country. Map data and the recipe corpus are Chinese. What ports is the pipeline — plug in your own map API (Google / OSM) and nutrition source (USDA / OpenFoodFacts), and keep the abstain layer.

Demo (no install): https://adexbn.github.io/walk-kcal/

同类赛道里，大多数项目只算了菜品的热量；提到「步行」的，几乎没几个把它接到热量上。

别人通常做的 我们做的 ------ 算一下麦当劳某一款汉堡的热量 你楼下的任意一家店，例如麦当劳 把距离当成「还有多远」 把距离换成「这顿能多吃多少卡」 只做一个平台 三种形式，共用同一份内核
