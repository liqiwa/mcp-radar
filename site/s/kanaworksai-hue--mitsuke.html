About mitsuke

Quick start · Use it from your agent · Web app · Commands · FAQ · ⭐ Star

Mitsuke 見つけ (Japanese for "find") is a local AI image and video search tool for artists, illustrators and filmmakers. Describe a picture in English, Japanese, Chinese or many other languages ("foggy bridge at dawn", "雪の中の鳥居", "云雾缭绕的水墨山水"), or show it another picture ("like this, but at night"). Mitsuke finds it in your own folders: photos, camera RAW, PSD, EXR / HDR, and the right shot inside your videos. It runs entirely on your Mac using Google's EmbeddingGemma 2, so your images never leave your computer.

Use it in a local web app, from the command line, or let your AI agent (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, …) search for you through MCP.

The web app searching a reference library of public-domain images. Ukiyo-e bridges show up next to the photos: the search goes by what is in the picture, not by file name.

🔎Search by meaningWords, an example image, or both, with a slider to balance them 🎞️Made for real librariesRAW, PSD / PSB, EXR / HDR, HEIC, and videos searched shot by shot 🤖Agent-readyMCP server, JSON CLI and a skill, so your agent can find images and video shots and show them to you 🔒LocalRuns on Apple Silicon with MLX. No account, no upload, no API key

- Keeps up with your folders: while the web app or mitsuke index --watch is running, new and changed files are indexed automatically. Moved and renamed files are recognized and keep their index entries. - Moodboards: collect results on moodboards, then use a whole moodboard as your query. - Never touches your files: Mitsuke only reads them.
