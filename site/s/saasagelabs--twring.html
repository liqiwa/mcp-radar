About twring

Call the AI agents you already run from your phone, and let them call you back. Your agent keeps its own model, memory and tools. Twring adds the call.

Twring in 30 seconds. Click for the full film.

Your agents are working. Why are you still watching them?

--- --- Talk to the agent you already run Press Call and talk to Hermes, Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI or any agent with its own web address. Same agent, same memory, same tools. It rings you when it matters When a job is done or needs your OK, your agent rings your phone, even when it is locked. A real call, not voice notes Streaming speech both ways, natural turn-taking, and you can interrupt at any moment. You stay in control Approvals arrive as a question on the call. Say yes or no, or tap. Twring never runs your agent's tools for it.

Voice is input and output, not intelligence. Twring turns your speech into text for your agent and its reply into speech for you, handles turn-taking and interruptions, and keeps the call alive across network changes. Everything your agent decides and does stays with your agent.

Use Twring's service &nbsp;(nothing to run)

1. Get an invite at twring.com and install the app (iPhone through TestFlight, Android from the download link). 2. Sign in, then connect your agent: the app shows the one command to run on the computer where your agent lives. 3. Press Call.
