About nightshift

A music-video crew that works the night shift on your own GPU. Song (lyrics and music), key frames for every shot, multiple takes, edit, grade, animated motion-graphics lyric HUD. Directed by you, or not.

Nightshift turns a song idea into a finished music video with open-weight models running in a local ComfyUI:

- MiniMax Music 3 writes and sings the song from a production brief and a lyric sheet - FLUX 3 Image Klein draws a seven-panel character sheet for each performer (front, 3/4, profile and back, plus three face angles), paints the first frames and keeps the faces and wardrobe consistent from shot to shot (Flux 2 Klein 9B, the first video's model, is still there: "imagemodel": "flux2", for its LoRAs) - LTX 2.3 animates them: lip-sync to the song, first-to-last-frame camera moves, text-to-video establishing shots - Python tools fit the shot list to the song, score every take against the character sheets, cut on the beat, grade, and draw a lyric HUD that steers around faces

Nightshift Studio (nightshift app) puts all of it behind one page in your browser: a photo, a song, a scene, a format, then Start the shift. Or an agent runs the pipeline over MCP (Claude, or any agent that speaks MCP, including one running on a local model), and the GPU works through the night. Direct every pass, or let it make the calls and wake up to a finished video.

The first video made with it, while the tools were being developed at the same time: "What Do You Want?" (2:49, 52 shots, nine passes), posted October 28, 2026.
