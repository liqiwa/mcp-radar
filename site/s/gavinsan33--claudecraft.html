About claudecraft

Play Minecraft with Claude. Type in chat and Claude builds, invents new items, blocks and creatures, and runs commands for you, live in your world.

Download the latest release · Minecraft 26.3 · Fabric

"Build the Eiffel Tower over there." Blocks go up in front of you with sounds and particles while Claude designs the rest. It shapes terrain, uses WorldEdit-style tools for domes, towers and curves, and looks at its own work to fix what's off.

A Taj Mahal, a giant donut and the White House, all built by Claude in one world.

Ask for something that doesn't exist and Claude designs it on the spot and adds it to your world. Everyone sees it right away, with no resource packs and no restart.

- Items. "Make me a frost wand that freezes water when I right-click." Claude draws it like a vanilla item and gives it its own powers: weapons, food, real pickaxes and axes, armor you can wear, bows and throwables, and abilities on right-click, on hit, or while held or worn. - Blocks. "Add ruby ore and a glowing mushroom lamp." You can place, mine and build with them like any other block. Ores can need the right pickaxe, drop gems and experience, and generate underground. Blocks can also face you when placed, store items, work with redstone, and grow or spread on their own. They can be animated too, like flickering fire that sets you alight or a shimmering portal you can step into. - Creatures. "Spawn a family of hippos by the river." Mobs that walk, fly, swim or hop, with their own health, behavior, sounds and drops. You can tame them, ride them, breed them for babies, and get spawn egg
