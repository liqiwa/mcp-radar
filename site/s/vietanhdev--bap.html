About bap

A cute, fast voice assistant firmware for the Seeed SenseCAP Watcher

The device talks straight to Google Gemini Live. No relay server, no cloud of our own.

Documentation · Demo · Features · Quick start · How it works · Controls

Recorded from the device's own screen (GET /screen.jpg), in real time.

Ask anythingIt answers with live subtitles, then plays the song Five charactersSwipe the screen or turn the wheel Round menuHold the wheel or the screen

23 expressions per character, drawn natively with LVGL, with lip sync while speaking

More screens: music, radio, menus, Korean and Chinese

Listening Speaking, live subtitles Zing MP3, synced lyrics Radio (VOV, FM Hà Nội)

Round menu Settings on the wheel Korean Chinese

🗣️ Talk naturally Real-time two-way audio with Gemini Live: interrupt anytime, live subtitles on the round screen. Double-tap the screen or press the wheel (or say "Hi Lili" once voice wake-up is on in the settings).

👆 Made to be touched Tap and it giggles, double-tap to talk, swipe to switch characters, hold for the menu. The scroll wheel does the same, plus volume.

👀 Notices you The camera's own AI chip spots a person and wakes the screen with a greeting, and a morning briefing once a day. No image leaves the device and no Gemini quota is used.

🎭 Five characters Bông, Bắp, Bơ, Mochi and Tí, each with a voice, personality and natively drawn face. Characters, apps and languages are plain data, so no C++ is needed to add one.
