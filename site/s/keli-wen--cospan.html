About cospan

Unified orchestration across coding agents. Connect Antigravity, Codex, Claude Code, DeepSeek Harness, and more through one async delegation protocol.

Why Cospan · Core CUJs · Install · Harnesses · Roadmap · References

Frontier coding models perform best inside the native harnesses they were co-trained with—so the real unit of orchestration is Model × Harness, and different ecosystems bring distinct strengths: Gemini 4 Carbon (agy) for architecture and planning, GPT-6.2-Sol (codex) for deep bug-hunting review, GPT-6.1-Terra (codex) for live GUI and browser computer use, and Claude (claude) for frontend craft. Combining them unlocks massive leverage; humans can bridge harnesses manually with handoff prompts today, but orchestration belongs to the agents themselves.

Inspired and battle-tested by agy-staff, Cospan turns cross-harness delegation into an async, progressively disclosed protocol behind one unified adapter: your host dispatches peer Model × Harness units in the background, stays unblocked with a clean context window, and pulls live status or full execution traces only when needed—forming a clean foundation for next-generation multi-agent collaboration.
