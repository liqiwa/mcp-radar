About zeus-options-terminal

Everything an options trader keeps ten browser tabs open for, in one terminal.

Live positions & Greeks · P/L and risk · exit discipline · paper trading with option and stock legs · a four-source news desk · stock picker · earnings & SEC filings · an honest trade journal. Connect tastytrade, Alpaca or Public, or upload a CSV from any broker. It runs entirely on your own computer, in 中文 / 双语 / English.

🌐 Website · 中文说明 → READMEzh.md · 🚀 Quick start · What's new · All features · User manual · Contributing

The trades in the tour are made up; quotes, news and charts are real data from the day it was recorded.

The first run installs everything into a local virtual environment (Python 3.10+) and opens http://localhost:8765. Then:

1. Press Load the sample to try every page with a sample portfolio priced from today's quotes, or 2. open ⚙ Settings → Broker & data and connect tastytrade, Alpaca or Public (each has a Test connection button), or upload a CSV of your trades. 3. News sources, AI and e-mail alerts are optional, in the same Settings page. Nothing is edited by hand.

Port 8765 taken? Run PORT=8790 ./start.sh. Where each broker's keys come from: Connect your broker.

One command and no clone. pipx puts ZEUS in an environment of its own (Python 3.10+):

Settings and data stay in ~/.zeus-terminal, so pipx upgrade zeus-options-terminal keeps them. pip install zeus-options-terminal inside a virtual environment works too. More in manual §1.8.
