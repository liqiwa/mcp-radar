About babelscribe

babelscribe — free, offline speech-to-text and subtitles on any GPU (AMD, NVIDIA, Intel, Apple)

babelscribe turns any audio or video file into subtitles (SRT, VTT) and text, in any of Whisper's 99 languages, on your own graphics card — AMD Radeon, NVIDIA, Intel Arc or Apple Silicon — or the CPU. No CUDA needed, no cloud, free (MIT). It runs OpenAI Whisper through whisper.cpp with Vulkan, CUDA or Metal, and works from the command line, from Python, or from AI apps (Claude, Codex, Antigravity, Gemini CLI, Cursor) as an MCP server.

babelscribe is a thin, friendly layer on top of whisper.cpp. It exists because getting fast Whisper on a non-NVIDIA card (e.g. an AMD Radeon on Windows) still means building whisper.cpp with the Vulkan SDK yourself. babelscribe downloads a prebuilt whisper-cli for your system and handles the rest.
